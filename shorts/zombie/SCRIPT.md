# "The Government's Zombie Plan" (original script v1, 2026-11-20)
Format: cartoon 3D story short, about 62 s at 3.7 words/s, second-person "if you woke up" mode, one steady narrator.

## Facts (all jokes come from these)
- **CONPLAN 8888-11, "Counter-Zombie Dominance"**: US Strategic Command, dated 30 April 2011, written by junior officers
  as a training exercise. It sorts zombies into classes: pathogenic, radiation, evil magic, space, vegetarian, chicken and others.
  - "Space zombies" are dead "zombie satellites", dangerous only if they de-orbit unexpectedly.
  - "Vegetarian zombies" eat only plants and are not dangerous.
  - "Chicken zombies": the plan says "although it sounds ridiculous, this is actually the only proven class of zombie that actually exists."
  - A fictional enemy was used so the training plan could not be mistaken for a plan against a real country (widely reported, 2014).
  - Sources: STRATCOM FOIA release CONPLAN_8888-11.pdf (stratcom.mil); Wikipedia "CONOP 8888"; offgridweb.com and history.com summaries.
- **CDC "Preparedness 101: Zombie Apocalypse"**: posted 16 May 2011 on the Public Health Matters blog by Rear Adm. Ali S. Khan.
  The blog normally drew 1,000-3,000 hits (per day according to Live Science, per week according to Wikipedia). A CDC tweet
  sent so many readers that the site crashed for hours. The kit it lists is water, food, medications, tools, sanitation, first aid,
  a radio and a flashlight: a normal emergency kit. Later in 2011 the CDC published a graphic novel, "Preparedness 101: Zombie Pandemic".
  - Sources: Wikipedia "Preparedness 101: Zombie Apocalypse"; Nextgov (May 2011); Live Science (May 2011); Harvard Health blog (May 2011).
- Unverified, so NOT used: exact hit counts (30,000 vs 60,000) and any claim that zombies "got more people prepared than hurricanes".

## Script (about 230 words)
| # | Beat | Line | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | If you woke up tomorrow in a zombie apocalypse, the United States military already has a plan for it. | You in pyjamas at a bedroom window; shambling green figures in the street below. Flash. |
| 2 | Setting | It's 2011, and in a quiet Strategic Command office, a few junior officers are writing a real document called Counter-Zombie Dominance. | Golden-hour concrete base, flag (Stars and Stripes), officers at a desk with a thick binder. |
| 3 | Action 1 | Page by page, they sort the undead into types. | Push-in on the binder, pages flipping. |
| 4 | Action 2 | Radiation zombies. Evil magic zombies. Vegetarian zombies, which only eat plants. | Glowing zombie; zombie with a wizard hat; zombie munching a hedge. |
| 5 | Action 3 | Space zombies, which turn out to be dead satellites, only dangerous if they fall on you. | A dead satellite tumbling in the sky, an officer looking up. |
| 6 | Snap | And chicken zombies, which the plan calls the only kind proven to actually exist. | A deadpan chicken stares down the lens. Kick shake. |
| 7 | Setting 2 | That same spring, the CDC, the people who fight real epidemics, posts a zombie survival guide on its blog. | Crossfade to a day office, a scientist at a computer. |
| 8 | Action 4 | Normally a few thousand people read it. This time so many show up, the website crashes. | A crowd of cartoon readers piles in front of a giant monitor; the monitor sparks and goes dark. |
| 9 | Deadpan double | Luckily, the government was ready for zombies. Unluckily, its website wasn't ready for readers. | Officers saluting / the smoking server. |
| 10 | You see | You see, nobody thought the dead would rise. A made-up enemy let planners practise without any real country thinking the plan was about them. | Night-blue explanation light, a map table with zombie pins instead of flags. |
| 11 | Analogy | And the CDC's list? Water, food, a flashlight, medicine, a first aid kit. Ever packed for a camping trip? It's the same bag you'd need for a hurricane, a flood or a blackout. | Items drop into a backpack one by one; the backpack sits next to a tent, then rain and a dark house. |
| 12 | Consequence | The CDC liked the reaction so much, it turned the guide into a comic book. | A comic book lands on the desk. |
| 13 | Callback | So if you wake up and the street is full of zombies, don't panic. Grab the camping bag, and keep an eye on the chickens. | Back at your window with the backpack on; the chicken is on the windowsill. End on the last word. |

Notes:
- Zombies are cartoon and silly (green skin, droopy eyes, arms out), never gory.
- No captions or labels. Signs and screens carry no readable text.
